Connect Playtomic once

  1. Install the GetACourt Reader extension v0.3.0 in Chrome/Edge once. In that same browser profile, open Playtomic and sign in normally.
  2. On an official Playtomic club page, click the GetACourt extension icon and choose Connect and open GetACourt. Approve Chrome's site-access request once. Your password and login cookies remain with Playtomic.
  3. After that, search here using Find courts. No extension clicks, copy/paste or repeated club-tab approval are needed while your Playtomic session remains valid. If Playtomic requests a new login or security check, complete it on Playtomic itself.

Search currently covers one approved Playtomic club at a time, not every club. Browser/extension must be running. Chrome may ask you to sign in again if Playtomic expires the session.

Enter search filters and click Find courts. GetACourt checks your existing provider session in this browser.

Manual Reader troubleshooting (not needed for normal searches)

If the automatic extension connection is unavailable, open Reader from its icon, search the club and choose Open GetACourt comparison. On this page choose Receive prepared Reader offers and deliver the prepared report from the extension icon.

In the Reader select Copy observed offers. Paste only the sanitized report below. Never paste a password, token, cookie, account export or customer information.
