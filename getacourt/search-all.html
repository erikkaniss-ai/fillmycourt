Use your private Playtomic reader

The personal reader opens inside the GetACourt extension. There are no IDs or pairing codes to copy, and this web page cannot read your provider session.

  1. Open the intended Playtomic club in the Chrome/Edge profile where you normally sign in.
  2. Click the GetACourt extension icon, then Connect and open GetACourt.
  3. Choose one day, sport and start-time range, then press Search this club.

Existing installations need one reload in Chrome's extensions page for version 0.2.3. Renew the club grant after a browser restart or lost access; no code is needed.

The reader uses the approved club's normal filters and offer disclosures in a compact window. It never presses Book or payment controls. Results expire after 60 seconds. Disconnect is available in both reader and popup.

Coverage is partial and provider freshness unknown. TiePlayer, Racket ID and Smashpro are not connected in this extension.
