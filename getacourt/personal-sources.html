Search clubs across sources — native Core offers and a clearly labelled public club directory.

Connect your Playtomic club

The personal reader now opens inside the GetACourt extension. There are no IDs or pairing codes to copy, and this web page cannot read the club.

  1. Open the intended Playtomic club in the Chrome/Edge profile where you normally sign in.
  2. Click the GetACourt extension icon, then Connect and open GetACourt.
  3. Choose one day, sport and start-time range, then press Search this club.

Existing installations need one reload in Chrome's extensions page for version 0.2.2. After that, reopen GetACourt through its icon. Renew the club grant after a browser restart or lost access; no code is needed.

Search uses the approved club's normal filters and offer disclosures in a compact window. It never presses Book or payment controls. Results expire after 60 seconds. Disconnect is available in both the reader and popup.

This is a bounded personal browser reader, with unknown provider freshness and partial coverage. TiePlayer, Racket ID and Smashpro are not connected here.
