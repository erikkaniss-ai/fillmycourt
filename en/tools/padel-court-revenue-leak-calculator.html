Padel court revenue leak calculator

This is not another construction ROI calculator. It estimates revenue already expiring inside an operating club — then separates empty capacity from the part that may actually be recoverable.

  • The model calculates theoretical monthly court capacity and applies average occupancy. It then isolates only the empty capacity that could plausibly respond to CRM, match formation, cancellation recovery or another intervention. Finally it applies a recovery rate and illustrative costs.
  • Illustrative model, not a forecast or guarantee. Defaults: €249/month SaaS, 8% performance fee on recovered gross and an illustrative 8% incentive cost. The Audit replaces these inputs with agreed real data.

How it works

capacity = courts × hours/day × 30.44 × average price
unsold = capacity × (1 − occupancy)
addressable = unsold × addressable share
recovered gross = addressable × recovery rate
net = recovered gross − incentives − performance fee − SaaS fee
