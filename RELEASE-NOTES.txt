FillMyCourt v3.3 — Revenue-first production release
Date: 19 Sep 2026

PURPOSE
This release promotes the reviewed redesign direction into the production-ready package while preserving the previous production release under /archive/v2/ and via its immutable Netlify deploy permalink.

POSITIONING
- Primary promise: fill empty/at-risk courts and recover incremental revenue automatically.
- FillMyCourt remains the B2B operating/revenue core, not the generic consumer brand.
- Booking, CRM, matchmaking, distribution, payments, analytics, player apps, media integrations and enterprise control exist underneath the Revenue Engine.
- Public content demonstrates the sophistication of the Demand Engine without publishing proprietary scoring weights, thresholds, exact timing, channel sequencing or incentive trees.

PRODUCT
- Revenue Map + financial simulator + owner P&L.
- Automated behavioural Player CRM.
- Demand Engine: Revenue Map → Audience Intelligence → Decision Layer → Activation → Attribution & Learning.
- Native booking/inventory core and connector architecture.
- External marketplaces/discovery channels can remain acquisition sources; direct repeat booking can grow where contracts, permissions and consent allow.
- Club white-label player app architecture with optional “Powered by FillMyCourt”.
- Separate international cross-club consumer network architecture; permanent consumer brand remains TBD and is not exposed as a fake launch brand.
- Match formation/open-match support.
- Media/performance integration layer for specialist video providers; provider references are examples/integration targets, not partnerships.
- Multi-location hierarchy, tenant isolation, role/policy model, API/webhook path and enterprise scale architecture.

COMMERCIAL
- Revenue Leak Audit: EUR 490 / five business days.
- Current Sprint hypothesis: EUR 1,250 setup + EUR 249/month + 8% of agreed attributed uplift.
- Financial examples remain illustrative, not client results or guarantees.
- Marketplace/platform fee savings are user-configurable illustrations and separated from payment-processing costs.

PLAYER BRAND ARCHITECTURE
- FillMyCourt = B2B platform / revenue engine.
- White-label Club App = club-owned player relationship and direct booking surface.
- International Booking Network = separate future consumer brand, name TBD.
- Portable/public identity is separated from tenant-private club CRM.

SEO / LLM / ANALYTICS
- Production canonical and hreflang restored.
- Indexable commercial pages included in sitemap.
- Demo and onboarding are noindex; historical /archive/ is noindex/noarchive.
- robots.txt allows public acquisition content and blocks archive/onboarding from crawlers.
- llms.txt and llms-full.txt describe the final revenue-first architecture and explicit limitations.
- First-party Netlify event layer retained for sessions, source/UTM, CTA, financial simulator, channel-margin interactions, lead/onboarding events and demo interactions.
- Netlify commercial lead/onboarding forms retained.

ARCHIVE
- Previous live production v2 is bundled at /archive/v2/ with noindex/noarchive, forms disabled and analytics disabled.
- Previous production immutable Netlify deploy: https://6aad86172d703ba021f786db--fillmycourt.netlify.app/

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- No universal external booking integration is claimed.
- No provider partnership is claimed unless separately verified.
- No global multi-region SLA is claimed before implementation/validation.
- No guaranteed revenue/ROI/search ranking/LLM visibility claim.
