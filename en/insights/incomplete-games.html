Match formation

Why three players can be as costly as an empty court.

In a four-player sport, a 3/4 group can be a booking at risk. Filling one place may protect the whole game.

The asset is not only the court

A club can have availability and still lose revenue because the group cannot complete the game. Match formation belongs in demand management, not only community features.

Compatibility beats reach

The best fourth player is not “anyone available”. Level, time, partner history, frequency and format preference can improve acceptance and reduce dropouts.

Attribute value carefully

If three players have already paid, adding a fourth does not automatically mean the full court value was recovered. The right metric depends on payment model and the real cancellation risk.

Make the workflow repeatable

Detect 3/4, select candidates, send the opportunity, confirm, and record the effect. Once repeatable, the process no longer depends on the receptionist’s WhatsApp group.
