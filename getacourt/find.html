Search using your Playtomic browser session

  1. In the same Chrome or Edge browser profile, sign in to the official Playtomic website if needed. Open a club page. Example: Cascais Padel & Nature.
  2. Click the GetACourt Reader extension icon, select Connect and open GetACourt and search the same date, sport and time range. Do not click Book or Continue.
  3. In the Reader, select Open GetACourt comparison. On this page, set your filters and choose Receive prepared Reader offers; then click the extension icon here and select Deliver prepared Reader offers here.
