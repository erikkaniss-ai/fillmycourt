FAQ

The questions that matter before changing your stack.

Do I have to replace my current booking system?

No. FillMyCourt can start as a revenue/CRM layer over the current stack. Native booking and migration become options when they improve control, margin or experience.

Does staff need to operate another CRM?

The goal is the opposite. Segments, opportunity queues and routine actions update automatically; the team supervises exceptions and results.

Can I keep using marketplaces?

Yes. The strategy is to keep channels that bring incremental demand and increase direct repeat booking where contracts, APIs, consent and economics justify it.

Do provider names imply partnership?

No. They are examples of systems relevant to connector/integration targets. We only communicate a partnership or live integration after validation.

Does the Audit guarantee a return?

No. The Audit exists to measure the economic case before installing workflows or promising uplift.
